Research Fellow at the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**, specializing in **AI-Driven Materials Discovery**. 

My research integrates **multi-fidelity machine learning techniques** with advanced quantum chemistry (TD-DFT, CASSCF/CASPT2, nonadiabatic dynamics) to accelerate the design of photofunctional materials, organic fluorophores, and imaging agents. By combining high-throughput computational screening with AI, I aim to bridge the gap between theoretical molecular design and real-world biomedical and energy applications.
