Aug 2026

Multicolour fluorogenic peptides in Nature Chemistry

Our collaborative work, “A general strategy towards multicolour fluorogenic peptides for wash-free bioassays”, was published online in Nature Chemistry.

Research Fellow at NUS I-FIM

I joined NUS I-FIM as a Research Fellow, working on delta machine learning within the NRF Materials Data Foundry project. About the Materials Data Foundry.

FB3 2026 in Torino: presented and connected

Shared work and met colleagues at the 6th International Conference on Fluorescent Biomolecules and their Building Blocks in Italy, with support from NTU and MOE Singapore.

2024

Celebrating My PhD Graduation in Computational Chemistry!

Successfully completed PhD in Science, Maths and Technology at SUTD, focusing on excited-state conformational dynamics of organic dyes.

2024

Exciting News: Bursary Award for Summer School in Catalysis!

Awarded a bursary to attend a summer school in catalysis, furthering research in computational chemistry applications.

2024

Oral Presentation at Fluorescent Biomolecules Conference

Delivered an oral presentation at the Fluorescent Biomolecules conference in Hong Kong, sharing research on photostability and excited-state dynamics.

12th Asian Photochemistry Conference in Melbourne
